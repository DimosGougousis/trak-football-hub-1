# Games 3/8 (TRAK-285) plan: plan step 2, line-up and jobs

**Issue:** TRAK-285 (spec TRAK-282). **Design:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-games-tab-design.md`.
**Branch:** `TRAK-285-line-up-and-jobs` from main. One PR, a draft until complete.

**Decisions (Imad, 28 Oct, question tool):**

- A player who can come and isn't on the pitch starts on the **bench**. Leaving a child out is always a deliberate tap.
- **Formations:** one list per format, used for ours and for theirs (the design doc lists them).
- **Bench or Not selected** lives in the player's sheet, as a radio-group. Arc's radio-group only stacks, so it can't
  sit inline in the list.

**Arc:**

- **New to Trak:** radio-cards, combobox, chip-group, expandable-text and progress. All are free, so `PRO.md` doesn't
  change.
- **React 18 fixes:** chip-group passes `ref` as a prop to Chip and MoreChip, which is React 19 only. Those two, and the
  `Swap` helpers in chip-group, progress and badge, now take it through `forwardRef`. Without that, AnimatePresence's
  `popLayout` can't measure them.
- **chip-group stays for context,** though Arc's rule says "checkbox in forms": the five tags read as tags and must fit a
  phone sheet.

## Tasks

1. **Migration `20261028140000_game_plan_players.sql`:**
   - `game_plans` gains `format` (default 11) and a `formation` that must fit it.
   - New table `game_plan_players`: role, slot, job, note, context, expectation. A player left out keeps the note only.
   - A slot holds one player. The check runs at the end of each statement, so two starters can swap in one save.
   - Rows are written only for consented children on the coach's roster in the match's squad. Writes need the games
     switch and an open match. No deletes.
   - **Suite:** `supabase/tests/games_plan_players.sql`, 40 checks.

   *Verify:* `node scripts/test-db.mjs --games-plan-players`. Four weakened copies of the migration must each fail it.

   - `src/lib/games/formations.ts`: formats, shapes, slot positions and line names.
   - `src/lib/games/lineup.ts`: place, take off, bench or not selected, format changes, the progress count, and the
     rows to save.

   *Verify:* `lineup.test.ts`.
3. **Screens:**
   - `GamePlan` holds the stepper, Back and Next, and one Save draft for both steps.

     selected for a player.
   - Who can come reuses Squad › Availability's reads (`availability-reads.ts`).

   *Verify:* `game-lineup-trak285.test.tsx`, and the TRAK-283 and TRAK-284 screen tests.
