# Games 4/8 (TRAK-286) plan: share the plan, and the player's and parent's view

**Issue:** TRAK-286 (spec TRAK-282). **Design:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-games-tab-design.md`.
**Branch:** `TRAK-286-share-the-plan` from main. One PR, a draft until complete.

**Decisions (Imad, 28 Oct, question tool):**

- **Share now is held back only by the logistics and the face-to-face tick.** Line-up complete and every starter
  having a job are checks the coach sees, not blocks. Arc's block holds the action until every check is done.
- **"Updated" lasts until kick-off.** Nothing records who opened what.
- **The coach's note is a tinted Trak panel,** like the panels around it, not Arc's `card`. Arc's card is for
  browsable items, lifts on hover, and its title was 20 px beside 13 px panels.

**Conflict, the issue wins:** TRAK-286 says a player reads "the goals" with no exception, so a player left out reads
them too. The design doc had said goals only when called up. Imad was told.

**Arc:**

- **Pro:** `release-readiness`, adapted as `blocks/release-readiness/share-readiness` and listed in `PRO.md`. The
  coach ticks one check; the other three are worked out from the plan and show a mark. No owners and no notify switch.
- **Free, new to Trak:** `checkbox` and `alert`. Alert's `Swap` takes its ref through `forwardRef` (React 18), as
  badge's does.
- **Already in Trak:** `segmented-control` (As the player / As the parent, a view mode), `select`, `badge`, `button`.
