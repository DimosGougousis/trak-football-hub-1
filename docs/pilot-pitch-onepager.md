# Trak — Get Better at the Game. Grow as a Person.
### A pilot proposal for a development tool that promotes values

---

**The idea in one line:** Trak helps young athletes track their progress and build good
habits — and, in the same loop, promotes the values of good sportsmanship. Performance is the
hook; values ride along.

---

## The problem

Kids tune out values lectures. Performance apps ignore character. Schools are left without a
single way to do both — through something kids actually enjoy. Yet sport is one of the best
places on earth to practise composure, respect, and effort under real pressure, every week.

## What Trak does

- **Improve (the hook):** athletes log matches, track progress, and stay organised — the
  reason they open the app daily.
- **Reflect:** a ~2-minute end-of-session habit covering *how they played* and *how they
  handled it* (a bad call, a loss, a teammate's mistake).
- **Recognise:** coaches celebrate great performances *and* great sportsmanship alike.

Values come woven into the loop kids already want to use — never a separate chore.

## The Trak Character Profile

A shared language of six values — practised through football, carried into life. Inspired by
the IB Learner Profile and built on established sport-education frameworks (Hellison's TPSR,
PCA's ROOTS). *Draft, to be validated with your educators.*

**Composure · Respect · Effort · Responsibility · Care · Transfer**

## What we promise — and what we don't

We are honest about what a tool can do:

- **We say:** helps athletes improve on and off the pitch; builds habits of reflection and
  organisation; promotes good sportsmanship; built on established frameworks.
- **We don't say:** that it "fixes" behaviour, guarantees character change, or solves any
  wider problem. It promotes and supports — it does not promise outcomes.
- **And:** character is never graded, scored, or ranked — it's a personal growth journey, never
  a comparison between children.

## What the pilot looks like

- **Scope:** one cohort, one term. Football-only.
- **Teacher burden:** minimal — students self-reflect; teachers facilitate lightly and view trends.
- **Risk to the school:** low and bounded — a single cohort, a clear before/after.

## Data & safeguarding

Children's behavioural data, treated accordingly:

- **GDPR-aligned** — data minimisation, parental consent, no public ranking or sharing.
- **Private by default** — reflections stay between student and facilitating teacher (parents
  later, only with consent).
- **Right to be deleted** — built-in account/data deletion tooling.

## How we'll measure success — *together*

- Reflection completion rate over the term.
- Self-reported progression across the values.
- Teacher-observed change in behaviour and sportsmanship.
- Qualitative feedback from students and teachers.

*Set with your educators before we start — and the values framework validated with an
educational/sport psychologist. We measure to learn, not to claim.*
