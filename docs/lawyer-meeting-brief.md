# Trak — Becoming Official: Legal Questions for Discussion

*Prepared for a meeting with a Greek lawyer. Last updated: 2026-07-10.*

## 1. Context

- **Trak** is a mobile-first web app for developing young football players. Coaches log
  sessions and assess players; players log matches and daily wellness; parents have
  read-only visibility. A **character-building feature** for children is in design.
- The data is sensitive by nature: **personal data of minors** (performance ratings, daily
  mood entries, soon behavioural self-reflections). Hosting/database providers are
  US-based (Supabase, Lovable).
- A **pilot is planned in Greece — either with a private school or a football academy.**
- **Current status: nothing is formalised.** No legal entity, no terms and conditions, no
  privacy policy, no signed agreements with the engineer or advisors, no trademark.
- **Goal of this meeting:** what it takes to establish the company, operate legally, and
  be covered — process, documents, and costs.

## 2. Creating the company

> **Discuss:** **What entity and where** — Greek IKE / other Greek form, or another EU
> option? What fits a software company whose first customers are Greek?

> **Discuss:** **The formation process, step by step** — documents, notary, tax registry,
> bank account, timeline. What can be done remotely, and what needs presence or a proxy?

> **Discuss:** **Costs** — realistic all-in setup cost, and what a small company costs
> **per year** to run (accountant, filings, social-insurance contributions, any minimum
> taxes even with little or no revenue).

## 3. Being covered

> **Discuss:** **Terms and conditions + privacy policy** — the minimum set before real
> users. What must they contain given users include children, coaches, and parents — and
> given the app offers behavioural/character content to minors (we make no therapeutic
> claims)? English or Greek?

> **Discuss:** **Personal liability** — until the company exists, everything happens in a
> personal capacity. What does the entity actually shield, and what remains personal?

## 4. Children's data (GDPR)

> **Discuss:** What's required to process minors' data lawfully — **parental consent**
> (from what age can a child consent alone in Greece?), whether mood/behavioural entries
> count as **special category** data, and any obligations that follow (e.g. DPIA).

> **Discuss:** Coaches can add a child to a roster (name, age) **before** the child or
> parent has an account — is that lawful, and if not how do we restructure it?

> **Discuss:** Our providers are **US companies** — what needs to be in place for the data
> of Greek minors?

## 5. The pilot

> **Discuss:** Does **school vs football academy** change the legal setup? Who is
> controller vs processor, who collects parental consents, and what must the **pilot
> agreement** contain at minimum?

## 6. Intellectual property

> **Discuss:** Our understanding is that an app **idea cannot be patented** in Europe
> (software/business methods are excluded) — correct? If so, what is the **strongest
> realistic protection**: trademark, copyright, contracts?

> **Discuss:** **Trademark "Trak"** — Greek vs EU registration, cost, and urgency.

> **Discuss:** **IP assignments** — code is written with help from a software engineer and
> a psychologist advises on content; nothing is signed. What must be signed so all IP
> belongs to the company, and can past work be assigned retroactively?

## 7. Priorities to settle in the meeting

1. **What entity, where, and what does it cost** — setup and per year?
2. The **formation process**, and what can be done remotely?
3. The **minimum protection package** (T&Cs, privacy policy) before real users?
4. Is our **children's-data model** lawful as designed, and what must change?
5. **Trademark + IP assignments** — what to sign now, and can it cover past work?
6. Which documents are **must-haves before the pilot**, their cost, and timeline?

## 8. The ask

A prioritised path — do now vs later, with rough total cost — and whether they can handle
the formation and drafting, on what fee basis.
