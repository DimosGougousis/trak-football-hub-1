The Problem

Youth football coaching runs on
WhatsApp groups and memory.

No structured records

Coaches have no way to track player development over time. Notes live in their head or a WhatsApp message.

Players get no real feedback

After a match, a player might hear "good game." There is no structure, no specifics, no path to improve.

Parents are in the dark

Parents pay club fees and drive to every session with no visibility into how their child is actually developing.

The tools professional clubs use cost thousands. Nothing exists for the grassroots level.

AI-Powered Feedback

Coach writes two lines.
Player gets a development plan.

The coach notes two or three things a player needs to work on after an assessment. Trak's AI expands those notes into three structured feedback points, each with a clear drill the player can practise.

Then the player can chat with the AI to ask follow-up questions: "How do I practise this alone?" or "Why does this matter for my position?" with full context from their coach's original notes.

Coach writes: "First touch under pressure, needs to check shoulder before receiving."

IMPROVEMENT POINT 1

First touch under pressure

When pressed in central areas, your first touch gives possession away. Angle your body before the ball arrives so your touch goes into space, not back to the defender.

TRY THIS DRILL

Find a wall, throw the ball and control every bounce with a single touch, directing it away from the wall. Ten minutes per session.

The Full Ecosystem

Every stakeholder
gets what they need.

Parents

See match results, read assessment summaries, get notified when their child wins an award. Transparent but coach-controlled. Parents see what coaches choose to share, nothing more.

Club Admins

A full academy dashboard across every coach and squad. A Movement Radar automatically surfaces players who are consistently outperforming their age group, flagged for promotion consideration.

Connected by design

Players join via a coach invite code. Parents are linked when players sign up. Clubs see all coaches under their academy. One action connects the whole chain.

Built for mobile

Coaches assess at the final whistle. Players check their card on the bus home. Works on any device, no app store required.

Where This Goes

The infrastructure layer for grassroots football development.

Every player who ever played youth football should have a record of it. Every coach should have the tools to develop players properly. Trak is the platform that makes that possible, starting with one coach and a squad of fifteen.

01

Start with the coach

Land with grassroots coaches. Make their life easier. They bring their players. Players invite parents. One coach brings fifteen to twenty users with them.

02

Grow into the club

When a club has five or more coaches on Trak, the admin dashboard sells itself. Academy-wide data in one place, for the first time.

03

Become the player's record

A player's Trak Passport follows them their entire career, from U8 to senior football. Every assessment, every award, every band.
