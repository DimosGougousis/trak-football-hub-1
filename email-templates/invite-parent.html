Your approval is needed

Your child signed up for TRAK, the app their football academy uses to record how they’re developing. They’re under 15, so nothing can be recorded about them until a parent or guardian approves it.

What you’ll be asked

To confirm you’re their parent or guardian, and to choose what their coach may record — their training assessments, any awards, and whether you can see their progress.

Each choice is separate. You can decline any of the optional ones, and it will not affect your child’s place at the academy or how their coach treats them.

Review and approve

You’ll set a password and create your own parent account. It is separate from your child’s — you will not be signed in as them. You can withdraw your approval at any time from your profile.
